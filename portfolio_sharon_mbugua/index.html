Sharon W. Mbugua combines clinical rigour with genuine warmth to help you move from confusion to clarity — and from coping to truly thriving. Her approach blends Person-Centred Theory, Acceptance & Commitment Therapy, and Cognitive Behavioural Therapy into a personalised roadmap designed entirely around you.

With deep expertise across the adult life span, Sharon is accredited by USIU-Africa, licensed by the Counsellors & Psychologists Board of Kenya (CPB), and holds an AoEC Certificate in Executive Coaching. She is also Hogan Assessment Certified (HAC) and a Global Arts in Medicine Fellow (2024).
